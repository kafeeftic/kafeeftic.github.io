How to Play Mega Tic-Tac-Toe

Mega Tic-Tac-Toe is a 3x3 grid of standard 3x3 Tic-Tac-Toe boards. Winning 3 small boards in a row wins the main game!

1
First Move: Player 1 places 'X' anywhere in any of the 9 sub-boards.
2
Directed Play: The specific cell you choose dictates where your opponent MUST play next! (e.g., Playing in the top-right cell of a sub-board forces opponent into the top-right sub-board).
3
Claiming Boards: Get 3-in-a-row inside a sub-board to claim that entire board for X or O.
4
Wildcards: If you are sent to a sub-board that is already won or full, you get a 🌟 Wildcard and can play in ANY open board!
